# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview 
This Project contains an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** designed to help organizations discover hidden insights within their workforce data. 
## Objectives of the Project
The primary objective is to track key performance indicators (KPIs), analyze employee attrition patterns, evaluate departmental performance, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve employee retention and satisfaction. The Project delivers a comprehensive look at demographics, compensation, tenure, and satisfaction levels to understand why employees are leaving and where interventions are most urgently needed. 

## Key Metrics & KPIs
1. Total Employees: 1,480  
2. Active Employees: 1,242
3. Attrition Count:  238
4. Attrition Rate:  16.08%
5. Average Age: 36.92 years old
6  Average Tenure: 7.01 years 
7. Average Monthly Salary: $6.50K 

## Core Insights & Visualizations 

### 1. Attrition Analysis by Demographics 
1. By Department: Research & Development experiences the highest chunk of total attrition in percentage (56), followed by Sales (39) and Human Resources (5). 
2. By Gender: Male employees account for a significantly higher proportion of attrition (63 percent, 151 count) compared to female employees (37 percent, 87 count). 
3. By Age Group: Attrition peaks sharply in the **18–25 age bracket** (reaching the highest overall *rate* of attrition), while the **26–35 age bracket** holds the highest absolute *count* of employees leaving. 

### 2. Attrition by Compensation & Role 
1.Salary Slab: Employees earning **Upto 5k/month** represent the highest risk group, suffering the largest number of departures (163). As salary slabs increase, attrition counts drastically decline. 

2.Job Role & Satisfaction Matrix: A detailed cross-tabulation mapping Job Roles against Job Satisfaction scores (1 to 4). **Laboratory Technicians** (62 total), **Sales Executives** (58 total), and **Research Scientists** (47 total) show the highest overall attrition, heavily concentrated among low-satisfaction scores. 

### 3. Education & Business Travel Trends 
1. Education Field: Employees with backgrounds in **Life Sciences** (41 percent) and **Medical** (32 percent) make up the vast majority of the tracked workforce distribution. 
2. Business Travel: Employees who Travel Rarely make up the highest absolute volume of attrition (157), followed by frequent travelers (69).

## Interactive Filters Available
Users can dynamically slice and dice the data using the right-hand filter pane to uncover specific sub-trends: 
Department, Age Group, Education Field, Gender, Marital Status, Business Travel Frequency, Salary Slab 

## Key Recommendations
1. Re-evaluate Entry-Level Compensation: Because the "Upto 5k" salary slab accounts for the vast majority of attrition, reviewing the competitive market rate for entry-level roles is crucial.
2. Targeted Retention for Early Careers: Focus retention programs, mentorship opportunities, and clear career paths toward the **18–35 age groups, where turnover is concentrated. 
3. Role-Specific Interventions:** Address the work environment and specific stressors impacting **Laboratory Technicians** and **Sales Executives** who report low job satisfaction scores.
